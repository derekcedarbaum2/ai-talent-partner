# Fictional application packet

Every person, employer, accomplishment and role below is invented for this example. The generator must use the real applicant's accomplishment bank when preparing a real application.

## Selected opportunity

Northstar Workshop is seeking a Product Manager for its maintenance scheduling product. The fictional posting asks for workflow discovery, product prioritization and experience improving completion rates.

The applicant marks **Yes** in the tracker. The generator prepares the following files for review.

## resume.md

Maya Chen
Product Manager

At Harbor Tools, I led discovery with 12 maintenance coordinators and worked with engineering to replace a manual scheduling spreadsheet. In the pilot, completed work orders rose from 68% to 81% over eight weeks.

## cover-letter.md

Hello Northstar team,

I have worked on the scheduling problem your team is hiring for. At Harbor Tools, I interviewed 12 coordinators, defined the first release, and worked with engineering through an eight-week pilot. Completed work orders rose from 68% to 81%.

I would bring that experience to your maintenance scheduling product. I start with the decisions coordinators make each day and test whether the product helps them complete more work.

Maya Chen

## application-questions.md

**Describe a product decision you made using customer research.**

The coordinators initially asked for more calendar filters. Interviews showed that missing parts caused most schedule changes. We prioritized a parts-readiness field and a queue of jobs ready to start. The completion-rate figures in my resume come from the resulting pilot.

## Review before sending

The applicant checks every claim against the accomplishment bank, verifies the posting is still open, and edits the materials. PDF rendering is optional. The tool never submits the application.
